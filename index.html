Welcome to moonmade.

I’m Jess Kowalski, the founder and creative heart behind moonmade. A vision three years in the making, inspired by the romance of Paris, the unexpected, and the art of finding beauty in what already exists.

Created in Canada, with a European sensibility, moonmade is an exploration of unique fabrics, thoughtful design, and pieces reimagined for a second life. Each creation is guided by an appreciation for the timeless, the whimsical, and the quietly extraordinary.

All my love,
Jess
