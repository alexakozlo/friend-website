About

Meet the maker

Welcome to MoonMade, I'm so happy you're here! I'm Jess Kowalski, and I make every bag you see here myself. I studied at fashion school in Italy, where I learned to make pieces with real care and quality, and I've loved it ever since.

I use good-quality upcycled materials and give them a sweet second life, and make each design in small batches, so there are only ever a few of each. Each one is made slowly, by hand, with a lot of love.

I hope you find one that feels like it was made just for you. ☾ Jess
